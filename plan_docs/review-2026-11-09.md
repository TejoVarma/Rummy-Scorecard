## Rule choices (Tejo to decide, not bugs)

- **Dealer re-deals if the player after them goes out** (`seating.ts`). This is intentional (commit `5400907`). code-reviewer called it non-standard. Keep it, but document it, or make it a setting.
- **Drop values default to 24/48/80 for every pool size.** Common conventions are 20/40 for 101 and 25/50 for 201. Consider per-preset defaults.
- **Tie-break when everyone goes out:** the lowest score wins, and an exact tie goes to the first seat.

## Disagreement with a reviewer

code-reviewer said `RE_ENTER_PLAYER` "has proper guards". It doesn't. It never checks that the player was eliminated in the last round, or that the re-entry score is below the pool limit. typescript-reviewer confirmed this (#10).
